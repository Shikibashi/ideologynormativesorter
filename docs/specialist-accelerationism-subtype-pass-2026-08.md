# Accelerationism subtype measurement pass — 2026-08

The technology-governance module previously contained one broad accelerationist candidate and one strategy item. That was too coarse for a term whose scholarly uses distinguish left, right, unconditional, and technology-centered directions.

The module now adds a separate experimental `market-acceleration` construct. Its item contrasts intensifying market competition with redirecting technological and economic capacity toward post-capitalist ends. The roster includes:

- Left Accelerationism;
- Right Accelerationism;
- Technology-Centered Accelerationism;
- the existing broad Accelerationism candidate;
- Cyberocracy, Techno-Anarchism, Dataism, and Technocratic Centralism as neighboring candidates.

This is a measurement distinction, not a claim that these are mutually exclusive identities. The module can still abstain, and every result remains experimental until cognitive interviews, reliability, test–retest, criterion, and fairness evidence support stronger interpretation.

The item is informed by [_Editorial Introduction: Accelerationism and the Left_](https://www.tandfonline.com/doi/full/10.1080/0969725X.2019.1568729), which describes disagreement among left, right, and unconditional variants over whether acceleration leads beyond capitalism or deeper into it. The source supports the boundary and construct distinction; it does not validate the application’s candidate profiles.
