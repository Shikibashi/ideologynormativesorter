# Question-context twenty-third pass

This pass uses `2026-08-question-context-v28` and adds bespoke context/source
records for all 46 specialist questions that previously used domain fallback
notes. The records cover the feminist, identity-sovereignty, anarchist,
green, socialist, conservative, religious-national, technology, and
monarchist/municipal experimental waves. They preserve experimental status
and do not alter assignment, evidence thresholds, prompts, response scales,
or ordinary primary scoring.

## Scope

- Feminist faction: `fm-fem-2`, `fm-fem-3`, `fm-fem-4`, `fm-fem-8`.
- Identity-sovereignty: `fm-id-1`–`fm-id-18` except retired or already-sourced records.
- Anarchist families: `fm-an-1`–`fm-an-4`.
- Green morphology: `fm-gr-1`–`fm-gr-4`.
- Socialist families: `fm-so-1`–`fm-so-4`.
- Conservative variants: `fm-co-1`–`fm-co-4`.
- Religious and national politics: `fm-rn-1`–`fm-rn-4`.
- Technology and governance: `fm-te-2` and `fm-te-4`.
- Monarchist and municipal/confederal: `fm-mm-1`–`fm-mm-3`.

The notes keep specialist distinctions narrow. Civic and ethnic membership
are not treated as exhaustive opposites; social ownership is not treated as
one institutional design; degrowth and green-capitalist strategies are not
collapsed; religious influence is not equated with clerical supremacy; and
algorithmic performance is not treated as sufficient for public legitimacy.

## Source basis

The records reuse the catalog’s academic and institutional sources, including
the [SEP entries on Feminist Political
Philosophy](https://plato.stanford.edu/entries/feminism-political/),
[Nationalism](https://plato.stanford.edu/entries/nationalism/),
[Multiculturalism](https://plato.stanford.edu/entries/multiculturalism/),
[Environmental Ethics](https://plato.stanford.edu/entries/ethics-environmental/),
[Socialism](https://plato.stanford.edu/archives/fall2025/entries/socialism/),
[Federalism](https://plato.stanford.edu/entries/federalism/),
[Democracy](https://plato.stanford.edu/entries/democracy/index.html),
[Islamic Constitutionalism research](https://www.cambridge.org/core/books/abs/democracy-under-god/islamic-constitutionalism/3C82791964D0B1824113F0AC38CEDD1B),
[NIST’s AI Risk Management Framework](https://www.nist.gov/itl/ai-risk-management-framework),
and the [SEP entry on Authority](https://plato.stanford.edu/entries/authority/).

These sources provide conceptual and institutional framing; they do not
establish psychometric validity or determine a respondent’s answer.
