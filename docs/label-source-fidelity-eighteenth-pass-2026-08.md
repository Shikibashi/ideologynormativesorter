# Label source fidelity: eighteenth pass (2026-08)

This pass closes the remaining bespoke-source gap for the niche provisional specialist entries that were still represented only by their catalog prose. The sources are claim-matched: they support a historical or descriptive definition and boundary note, but they do not validate a centroid, respondent measurement, or policy effectiveness.

## Newly sourced provisional specialists

| Label | Source | Editorial boundary |
| --- | --- | --- |
| Paleolibertarianism | Complutense University of Madrid, *Murray N. Rothbard’s Paleolibertarianism* | A historically situated Rothbardian strategy, not all right-libertarianism or paleoconservatism. |
| Eco-Fascism | Cambridge, *Illiberal Environmentalism* | A contested analytic term for far-right uses of nature and environmental language; strong environmental regulation alone is not eco-fascism. |
| National Bolshevism; Strasserism | Elsevier, *Historical Legacies and the Size of the Red-Brown Vote* | Historically variable red-brown currents, not generic socialism or nationalism. |
| Techno-Anarchism | Wiley, *Anarchism and the Politics of Technology* | Anarchist technology politics can include abolition, guarded adoption, or promotion; it is not automatic techno-optimism. |
| Utopian Socialism | Cambridge, *Utopian Socialism and Social Science* | A nineteenth-century family of social-reconstruction projects, not one contemporary policy program. |
| Voluntaryism | *Journal of Libertarian Studies*, *Voluntaryism: The Political Thought of Auberon Herbert* | Distinguishes Herbert’s voluntarily funded minimal state from later anti-state and anti-electoral uses. |
| Stirnerism / Egoist Anarchism | Cambridge, *Egoism and Class Consciousness* | A philosophical and contested reception history, not a unified party platform. |
| Anarcha-Feminism | Cambridge, *Anarcho-Feminism in Late Victorian and Edwardian Britain* | A historically diverse synthesis of anti-authoritarian and feminist critiques of domination. |
| Bright Green Environmentalism | Cambridge, *Environmentalisms Clashing* | A broad innovation- and technology-oriented environmental current, distinct from ecomodernism and dark-green approaches. |
| Bleeding-Heart Libertarianism | *The Independent Review*, *Rawls and Bleeding Heart Libertarianism* | A contested research program joining economic liberty and social justice, not one settled welfare package. |
| Christian Reconstructionism | Oxford Academic, *Christian Reconstruction: R. J. Rushdoony and American Religious Conservatism* | A small theonomic/Reformed current, not mainstream Christian conservatism or Christian democracy. |
| Queer Anarchism | SAGE, *Bridging the Divide Between Queer Theory and Anarchism* | An intersection of queer critique and anti-authoritarian politics, not one required theory of gender, family, or organization. |

All thirteen entries remain `specialist` and `provisional`. Their source records are limited to `definition`, `descriptive`, and `boundary` scopes. They remain excluded from ordinary primary and modifier scoring; a source record makes the catalog inspectable, not empirically validated.

## Remaining research work

The next evidence step is construct validation, not more centroid prose: build and pilot respondent-facing modules for these families where the project intends to report a specialist result. In particular, eco-fascism and red-brown labels need careful safety/editorial review, while bright-green environmentalism, voluntaryism, Stirnerism, and queer/anarcha-feminism need subtype questions that distinguish them from neighboring broad families.
