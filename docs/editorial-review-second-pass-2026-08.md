# Ideology, explainer, and question review — second pass (2026-08)

## Scope and boundary

This pass reviewed the respondent-visible ideology catalog, three-layer explainers, generated term help, and the effective question bank after semantic overlays. It preserves all label IDs, question IDs, centroid values, and question axis weights. Copy, taxonomy metadata, active-review status, and short-form tier membership changed only where the existing data contradicted its own stated scope or a reviewed item was not reaching respondents.

This remains an editorial and content-validity review, not empirical psychometric validation.

## Resolved findings

### Ideology catalog

- `accelerationism` is now displayed as **Technology-Centered Accelerationism**, matching the current centroid’s own caution instead of presenting one synthetic centroid as the whole left/right/technology family. Neo-Reaction is no longer assigned to the generic label.
- `ecomodernist` now sits in a green/ecomodernist taxonomy and uses ecomodernism, ecological modernization, decoupling, and techno-optimism as its layer metadata rather than treating generic technocracy as entailed.
- `religious-nationalism` is now a cross-cutting modifier, and Theocracy is no longer assigned to every religious-nationalist variant.
- `political-islam` now uses neutral religious-political taxonomy and copy that covers diverse relationships between Islamic principles, legislation, elections, and state-building.
- `constitutional-monarchism` is no longer categorized as authoritarian by definition; its copy distinguishes ceremonial parliamentary monarchy from constitutionally constrained executive variants.
- Unsupported layer tags were removed from `georgism`, `internationalism`, `fundamentalist-theocracy`, and `traditional-monarchist`.
- Child schools were removed from `aliases` for `maoism`, `zionism`, `strasserism`, and `libertarian-municipalism`; genuine subtypes remain under `subTheories` where appropriate.
- Broad display-name overrides were removed from `technocratic-centralist` and `civil-libertarian-cosmopolitan`, so the public name no longer overstates what each synthetic centroid represents.

### Explainers and term help

- The three application-specific layers are now labeled **Foundational values / ideal legitimacy**, **Empirical beliefs / how institutions behave**, and **Applied policy / strategy**. The copy explicitly says “In this test” because normative theory ordinarily includes both ideals and questions about what ought to be done.
- Term guides no longer scan negated or comparator prose. This removes false guides such as Fascism for `national-traditionalist`, Liberalism for `fascist-authoritarian`, and Progressivism for `social-conservatism`.
- National Socialism now receives only its specific Nazi-ideology definition, not a contradictory generic Socialism definition.
- The Theocracy matcher was corrected, and direct ID-based definitions were added for high-confusion terms including neoreaction, distributism, deep ecology, objectivism, democratic confederalism, and libertarian municipalism.
- Layer summaries now include positions tied at the cutoff in a deterministic order instead of silently choosing by source-array order.
- Axis-pole wording was made layer-consistent for anti-domination, militarism/pacifism, public-choice skepticism, coordination optimism, coercion strategy, and regulation/deregulation.
- Question help now respects `theoryContext`: ideal prescriptive items say “under ideal conditions,” while nonideal items say “under current constraints.” Ambiguous words such as intervention, sanctions, planning, reform, and inflation are now domain-qualified; “material intensity” is distinguished from total material throughput.

### Effective question bank

- The statement-only `sq04` review is now applied to the same effective bank used by the app and scoring.
- Ten additional items were recorded as replacement-required: `q0002`, `q0078`, `q0103`, `q0109`, `q0143`, `q0196`, `q0277`, `q0409`, `sq11`, and `sq12`. The third-pass methodology review supersedes the temporary coverage-preserving decision: these items are now quarantined until separately identified replacements pass expert review and cognitive testing.
- Already-approved active items were promoted into the short forms without changing prompts or weights. Blitz is again 21 items with 7 normative, 7 descriptive, and 7 prescriptive items. Quick is 65 active items and has at least one item in every domain/layer pair.
- The effective bank version now includes the main semantic review, statement semantic review, and respondent-tier review versions.

## Deferred findings requiring respondent evidence

- Directional keying is severely imbalanced on several axes. In the reviewed extensive bank, all 92 `liberty-noninterference` loadings and all 61 `public-choice-skepticism` loadings point in one keyed direction; `anti-domination`, `moral-traditionalism`, `state-action-vs-exit`, and `regulation-vs-deregulation` are also highly asymmetric. New neutral counter-keyed items require cognitive interviews, piloting, method-factor checks, and differential-item-functioning analysis.
- `constitutional-monarchism`, `world-federalism`, and `indigenism` are mapped to broad modules that do not yet contain label-specific discriminators. They remain specialist/provisional rather than primary results until targeted items and thresholds are validated.
- The ten replacement-required items listed above need clean single-construct rewrites and cognitive review before the flawed versions can be deactivated without shrinking already-sparse construct coverage.
- Several active prompts remain candidates for neutral rewriting because of loaded contrasts or likely redundancy, including `q0211`, `q0238`, `q0297`, `q0298`, `q0306`, `q0318`, `q0346`, `q0380`, and the pairs `q0048`/`q0410` and `q0242`/`q0406`.
- No centroid or axis weight was changed. Centroid calibration, item reliability, dimensionality, temporal stability, criterion agreement, tier equivalence, and DIF still require real respondent data.

## Editorial references

- [An Ecomodernist Manifesto](https://www.ecomodernism.org/manifesto-english) centers technological and institutional decoupling rather than technocracy as a required form of rule.
- [International IDEA: Constitutional Monarchs in Parliamentary Democracies](https://www.idea.int/publications/catalogue/constitutional-monarchs-parliamentary-democracies) documents constitutional monarchy as compatible with a symbolic, non-executive head of state in parliamentary democracy.
- [Journal of Democracy: Islamist Parties and Democracy — Three Kinds of Movements](https://www.journalofdemocracy.org/articles/islamist-parties-and-democracy-three-kinds-of-movements/) supports treating Islamist political movements as internally diverse in their relationships to democracy and political violence.
- [Stanford Encyclopedia of Philosophy: Moral Phenomenology and Normative Ethics](https://plato.stanford.edu/entries/moral-phenomenology/#MorPheNorEth) notes that normative ethics includes both general moral theory and practical questions about what to do, motivating the application-specific layer labels.
