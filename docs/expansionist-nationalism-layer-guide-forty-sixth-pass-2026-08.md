# Expansionist Nationalism layer guide — forty-sixth pass (2026-08)

## Scope

This pass adds one descriptive-layer summary for `expansionist-nationalism`. It changes consumer-facing layer copy and its test only. No centroid, axis weight, question prompt, question selection, or scoring behavior changed.

## Layer decision

Expansionist Nationalism now has a descriptive summary stating a recurring expectation in expansionist projects: territorial enlargement or external influence can restore national strength, security, status, or historical unity. The summary immediately distinguishes those movement rationales from research findings, noting that expansion can intensify conflict and undermine security and that rationales and outcomes vary by case.

The descriptive layer therefore does not endorse expansion, treat territorial acquisition as inherently security-enhancing, or substitute realism as an explanatory theory. The normative and prescriptive layers remain separate.
