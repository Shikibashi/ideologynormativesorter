# Ideology term-guide eleventh pass

Date: 2026-08-11

## Scope

This post-release pass reviewed eight high-confusion libertarian, anarchist, nationalist, Turkish republican, and religious labels. It changes consumer-facing definitions and layer summaries only. No centroid, axis weight, question prompt, question selection, or scoring behavior changed.

## Direct term guides

- **Georgist Libertarian** now identifies the combination of libertarian self-ownership and voluntary exchange with equal claims to land or natural-opportunity value, usually implemented through land-value charges.
- **Anarcho-Capitalist** now states its right-libertarian program of private property, contract, and competing law or protection providers and notes that its classification as anarchism is disputed.
- **Anarcho-Communist** now states its stateless, classless, common-ownership program and explicitly distinguishes it from anarcho-capitalism on productive property and wage labor.
- **Bleeding-Heart Libertarianism** now identifies the academic current combining civil liberties and market institutions with concern for social justice and the least advantaged.
- **National Bolshevism** now identifies a historically variable set of nationalist and Bolshevik or revolutionary-socialist syntheses while stating that this catalog models the post-Soviet authoritarian nationalist current.
- **Kemalism** now names Atatürkism and lists the Six Arrows: republicanism, nationalism, populism, statism, laicism, and reformism.
- **Christian Reconstructionism** now identifies the small Reformed Protestant theonomic movement and distinguishes it from generic Christian conservatism and Christian Democracy.
- **Fourth Political Theory** now identifies Aleksandr Dugin’s author-specific anti-liberal project, its civilizational and multipolar framing, its unsettled status, and the scholarly dispute over its claimed break with fascism.

Each guide is keyed to the stable catalog label and shown alone, preventing a generic parent-family definition from displacing the doctrine the result actually names.

## Catalog-scope corrections

- National Bolshevism no longer collapses interwar German currents and the post-Soviet Limonov/Dugin milieu into one invariant far-right doctrine. Its description now says which variant the current centroid intends to model.
- Fourth Political Theory no longer presents a Eurasian empire as a complete, settled policy platform. It describes Dugin’s project as overlapping with Neo-Eurasianism while retaining the academic radical-right caution.

## Layer corrections

- Georgist Libertarian now has direct normative and practical summaries for self-ownership, equal claims to land value, private tenure, and land or resource rent.
- Anarcho-Communist now has a direct practical summary for stateless federation, common control, and need-based distribution.
- Bleeding-Heart Libertarianism now has a direct normative summary joining liberty with concern for disadvantaged people.
- Kemalism now has a practical summary tied to the Six Arrows rather than a generic nationalism fallback.
- Christian Reconstructionism now has a practical summary tied specifically to theonomic civil law.

No empirical-belief summary was invented for a label that lacks a common, testable descriptive doctrine.
