# Label-source fidelity: sixteenth pass (2026-08)

This pass closes the remaining source gap for context-only entries. Context labels are browsable interpretive references, not ordinary scoring endpoints; adding a source does not promote them into primary or modifier scoring.

## Added context sources

- **Constitutional Monarchism** — Cambridge constitutional history on hereditary monarchy operating through parliamentary, personal, and judicial rules.
- **Dataism** — Cambridge research on the belief in quantifying and tracking behavior, including the associated dataveillance and privacy concerns.
- **Fundamentalist Theocracy** — Oxford work on Shiʿi political theology, used as a specific case for separating theocracy, clerical authority, and political secularism rather than as a universal religious-government definition.
- **Liquid Democracy** — Oxford scholarship on open and digitally mediated democracy, with an explicit warning that digital tools do not guarantee accountability.
- **Radical Centrism** — University of Notre Dame historical context for the contested term “radical center.”
- **Singularitarianism** — peer-reviewed SciELO research describing it as a contested transhumanist stream tied to technological-singularity narratives and political projects, without treating inevitability as evidence.
- **Social-Investment State** — Cambridge policy scholarship treating social investment as a welfare-policy paradigm, not a replacement for income security, care, or disability protection.
- **Platformism** — a primary movement text for platformism’s own organizational claims; it is marked as `primary-text` and is not treated as neutral or outcome-validating evidence.
- **Panarchism** — Routledge scholarship on non-territorial and voluntary-governance traditions.
- **Transhumanism** — Oxford political-ideology scholarship emphasizing plural enhancement and posthuman imaginaries rather than one political program.
- **Universal Basic Income** — Oxford political theory distinguishing the proposal from its variable amount, funding, tax, and service design choices.

The only source scopes attached to these records are those directly supported by the source type. The primary platformist text is limited to definition and boundary claims; no context label is thereby scored.
