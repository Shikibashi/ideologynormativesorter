# Question-context nineteenth pass

This pass uses `2026-08-question-context-v24` and adds bespoke context/source
records to 42 active questions across welfare, labor, land/housing, and
money/banking. It clarifies the layer boundary and the mechanism or policy
scope without changing prompts, response scales, axis weights, or labels.

## Scope

- Welfare and redistribution: `q0061`–`q0066`, `q0073`, `q0074`, `q0076`, `q0077`, and `q0079` (`q0075` already had bespoke context).
- Labor and workplace governance: `q0081`–`q0085` and `q0094`–`q0099`.
- Land, housing, and Georgist claims: `q0101`, `q0102`, `q0104`–`q0106`, and active items `q0115`–`q0117` plus `q0119` (`q0118` is inactive and is not part of the context bank).
- Money and banking: `q0121`–`q0126`, `q0133`, `q0134`, and `q0137`–`q0139` (`q0123` already had bespoke context).

The pass keeps descriptive evidence separate from normative standards and
prescriptive instruments. For example, benefit cliffs are not treated as a
general argument against means-testing; labor exit is not treated as a full
substitute for collective bargaining; housing supply is not treated as the
only response to affordability; and monetary competition is not treated as a
guarantee of stability.

## Source basis

The records use claim-matched entries already maintained in the source catalog:
[Distributive Justice](https://plato.stanford.edu/entries/justice-distributive/index.html),
[Liberalism](https://plato.stanford.edu/entries/liberalism/),
[Program Recertification Costs: Evidence from SNAP](https://www.nber.org/papers/w27311),
the [ILO collective-bargaining and labor-relations](https://www.ilo.org/topics-and-sectors/collective-bargaining-and-labour-relations)
materials, [ILO worker cooperatives](https://www.ilo.org/worker-cooperatives),
NBER research on [housing supply and affordability](https://www.nber.org/papers/w33694),
the [Federal Reserve monetary-policy overview](https://www.federalreserve.gov/aboutthefed/fedexplained/monetary-policy.htm),
and financial-resolution guidance from the [FSB](https://www.fsb.org/2014/10/key-attributes-of-effective-resolution-regimes-for-financial-institutions-3/)
and [FDIC](https://www.fdic.gov/resources/resolutions).

These sources support definitions, mechanism boundaries, and cautious framing;
they do not establish the quiz’s psychometric validity or determine a
respondent’s normative preference.
