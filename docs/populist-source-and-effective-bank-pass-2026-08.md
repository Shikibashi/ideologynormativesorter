# Populist source and effective-bank pass — August 2026

This pass closes a source-fidelity gap for the public populist labels and adds a regression check for the respondent-facing core bank.

## Source repair

The public catalog now cites two Cambridge sources for right-wing populism and left-wing populism:

- Cas Mudde, “The Populist Zeitgeist,” _Government and Opposition_: https://www.cambridge.org/core/journals/government-and-opposition/article/populist-zeitgeist/2CD34F8B25C4FFF4F322316833DB94B7
- “The Anatomy of Populist Ideology: How Political Parties Define ‘the People’ and ‘the Elite’,” _European Journal of Political Research_: https://www.cambridge.org/core/journals/european-journal-of-political-research/article/anatomy-of-populist-ideology-how-political-parties-define-the-people-and-the-elite/530AC0E964E62C709929D155CE14CEBB

The first source is used for the thin-centred people-versus-elite core and its normative appeal to a general will. The second is used for the context-dependent construction of “the people” and “the elite,” including the tendency for left-populist constructions to emphasize economic exclusion and for right-populist constructions to emphasize cultural or nativist exclusion. Neither source is treated as proof that every right- or left-populist actor shares one complete policy program.

Agrarian Populism retains its Oxford source on producerist rural politics and now also receives the Cambridge thin-core source. Its catalog note continues to state that agrarian populism can combine with socialist, cooperative, racial-solidarist, or market currents.

## Effective-bank safeguard

The core respondent bank now has an explicit test that every active item has `reviewStatus: approved` after all semantic and editorial overlays. Known non-discriminating or double-barreled prompts remain retained for audit history but inactive in the effective bank. This is a pipeline invariant, not evidence that the active wording has passed cognitive interviews, psychometric validation, or criterion-validity testing.
