# Socialist variants term-guide twentieth pass

Date: 2026-08-11

## Scope

This pass reviewed four socialist-family labels that still fell back to broad
socialism definitions: Democratic Socialist, Market Socialist, Socialist and
Marxist Feminist Traditions, and Juche. It adds direct term guides, layer
summaries, and regression tests. It does not change centroids, axis weights,
question prompts, question selection, result eligibility, or scoring behavior.

## Direct term guides

- **Democratic Socialist** now identifies a commitment to democratic control
  or social ownership of major productive assets through democratic
  institutions and movements. The catalog keeps this stronger ownership
  commitment distinct from social democracy’s usual mixed-economy reform
  usage, while recognizing that the terms have overlapped historically.
- **Market Socialist** now combines social, public, or worker-cooperative
  ownership with market allocation or pricing. It is distinct from both
  private-capitalist ownership and command planning, with internal variation
  over firm governance, investment, and the public role.
- **Socialist and Marxist Feminist Traditions** now identify overlapping
  approaches that analyze gender domination together with capitalism, class,
  labor, property, and social reproduction. The combined specialist label
  does not claim that the traditions are identical.
- **Juche** now refers specifically to the DPRK/Kimist state ideology of
  political independence, economic self-reliance, and military self-defense,
  organized around a centralized party-state and supreme-leader sovereignty.
  It is not generic socialism or a general philosophy of personal
  self-reliance.

Each guide is keyed to the stable catalog id and shown alone, so a specific
result is not displaced by a generic parent-family definition.

## Layer summaries

All four labels now have explicit normative, descriptive, and prescriptive
summaries:

- Democratic Socialist emphasizes democratic social ownership or control of
  economic power rather than only regulating a capitalist mixed economy.
- Market Socialist emphasizes social ownership together with the coordinating
  role of markets and prices, without assuming either private capitalism or
  command planning.
- Socialist and Marxist Feminist Traditions emphasize the interaction of
  gender domination with class, property, paid work, unpaid care, and social
  reproduction.
- Juche emphasizes national autonomy, self-reliance, military independence,
  centralized party leadership, and the DPRK/Kimist state form.

These distinctions separate an ownership goal, an institutional allocation
model, a feminist political-economic analysis, and a specific national state
ideology. They are editorial descriptions of the catalog profiles, not claims
that every party, movement, or state using one of these names has the same
complete program.

## Source basis

- “Socialism,” _Internet Encyclopedia of Philosophy_, on social ownership and
  the difference between planning-based and market-based socialist models:
  https://iep.utm.edu/socialis/
- “Market Socialism,” _Encyclopedia of Modern Political Thought_, on combining
  social ownership with market allocation of commodities and labor:
  https://sk.sagepub.com/ency/edvol/embed/encyclopedia-of-modern-political-thought/chpt/market-socialism
- “Socialist/Marxist Feminism,” _Oxford Bibliographies in Literary and Critical
  Theory_, on introducing sex and gender as categories of capitalist
  oppression and analyzing women’s relation to patriarchal institutions and
  wage labor:
  https://academic.oup.com/reference/62382/reference-article-abstract/555032950
- “Gender and social reproduction: historical perspectives,” _American
  Journal of Public Health_ 92.6 (2002), on social reproduction as a feminist
  concept extending beyond production in the narrow Marxist sense:
  https://pubmed.ncbi.nlm.nih.gov/12316382/
- Hyeon-Kim Kim, “Juche (Self-Reliance) in North Korea,” _The Encyclopedia of
  Political Science_ / Wiley reference entry, on ideological self-reliance,
  political independence, economic independence, and military independence:
  https://onlinelibrary.wiley.com/doi/abs/10.1002/9781118430873.est0820
- Charles K. Armstrong, “Socialism in our own style,” in _North Korea_, on
  Juche as an ideology formulated and promulgated by the North Korean state:
  https://doi.org/10.1017/CBO9781139021692.006

The catalog’s decision to treat these as separate result profiles is an
editorial taxonomy choice informed by the sources. The sources do not imply
one universally settled classification for every socialist movement,
feminist tradition, market-socialist model, or interpretation of Juche.
