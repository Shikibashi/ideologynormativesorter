# Specialist context-precision pass — August 2026

This pass adds bespoke boundary notes to eight specialist catalog entries that
were already defined and source-backed but were still easy to conflate with
nearby traditions. The notes do not promote any specialist to ordinary scoring,
change a centroid, or claim psychometric validity.

## Corrections

- **Technocratic Centralism:** separates concentrated expert rule from evidence-
  based administration and general technocratic orientation.
- **Georgist Libertarianism:** distinguishes natural-opportunity rent claims
  from generic libertarianism and from every Georgist tax design.
- **Market Socialism:** distinguishes an economic-system family from a party or
  state model and from social democracy.
- **Ordoliberalism:** distinguishes constitutional market-order construction
  from laissez-faire, generic regulation, and one welfare-state program.
- **Democratic Confederalism:** identifies the specific Öcalan-linked project
  and avoids treating it as all confederalism, Kurdish politics, or local
  democracy.
- **Liberal Feminism:** notes internal disagreement over structural inequality,
  care, affirmative action, and formal equality.
- **Agrarian Populism:** separates rural producer identity and thin populist
  rhetoric from any one economic, class, racial, or institutional program.
- **Green Capitalism:** surfaces commodification, measurement, bargaining-power,
  and distributional questions rather than implying that markets alone solve
  ecological transition.
