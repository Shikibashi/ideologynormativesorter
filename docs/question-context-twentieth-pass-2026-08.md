# Question-context twentieth pass

This pass uses `2026-08-question-context-v25` and adds bespoke context/source
records to 57 active questions across civil liberties, immigration and
membership, religion/secularism, and family/gender. It clarifies construct and
layer boundaries without changing prompts, response scales, axis weights, or
label assignments.

## Scope

- Civil liberties and speech: `q0161`–`q0166` and `q0174`–`q0179`.
- Immigration and movement: `q0201`–`q0206`, `q0213`, `q0214`, `q0216`, and `q0219`.
- National identity and sovereignty: `q0221`, `q0223`, `q0224`, `q0226`, `q0233`–`q0236`, and `q0239`.
- Religion and secularism: `q0241`, `q0243`–`q0246`, `q0253`–`q0259`, and `q0404`.
- Family, gender, and feminism: `q0261`–`q0266`, `q0275`, `q0276`, `q0278`, `q0279`, `q0421`, `q0478`, and `q0479`.

Existing high-risk records—such as `q0171`, `q0207`, `q0217`, `q0222`,
`q0225`, `q0242`, `q0405`, `q0406`, `q0414`, `q0415`, and `q0417`—remain
unchanged and are not counted in this pass.

The framing keeps descriptive, normative, and prescriptive claims separate.
For example, a right to move is not itself a complete admission system;
religious freedom is not identical to state neutrality; local autonomy is not
identical to majority rule; and formal gender equality is not treated as proof
that informal hierarchy has disappeared.

## Source basis

The records use the existing source catalog, including the [International
Covenant on Civil and Political Rights](https://2covenants.ohchr.org/About-ICCPR.html),
the UN [Human Rights Committee privacy guidance](https://docstore.ohchr.org/SelfServices/FilesHandler.ashx?enc=NDHbUvPo0H0e6ReM%2BeoASfvbY3aH6JttLpsRvkmL87wD80W4xnAiRpmKA6ZLwTp9xzCLNUYwTBP35uH1Fw6yqQ%3D%3D),
the [SEP entry on Immigration](https://plato.stanford.edu/archives/win2024/entries/immigration/),
the [1951 Refugee Convention](https://www.unhcr.org/about-unhcr/overview/1951-refugee-convention),
[Nationalism](https://plato.stanford.edu/entries/nationalism/),
[Multiculturalism](https://plato.stanford.edu/entries/multiculturalism/),
[Religion and Political Theory](https://plato.stanford.edu/entries/religion-politics/),
[Feminist Political Philosophy](https://plato.stanford.edu/entries/feminism-political/),
and [Feminist Ethics](https://plato.stanford.edu/entries/feminism-ethics/).

These sources support definitions, rights boundaries, and cautious framing;
they do not establish psychometric validity or determine a respondent’s
answer.
