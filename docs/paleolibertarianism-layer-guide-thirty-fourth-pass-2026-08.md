# Paleolibertarianism layer guide

Date: 2026-08-11

## Scope

This focused editorial pass adds the missing descriptive layer summary for
`paleolibertarianism`. Its direct term definition and normative or practical
summaries were already specific, but its descriptive layer fell back to
generic libertarian text. The new copy treats Paleolibertarianism as a
historically specific coalition and strategy rather than a generic synonym
for libertarianism. No centroids, axis weights, question prompts, or scoring
behavior changed.

## Layer decision

**Paleolibertarianism** now describes the expectation that welfare-state
expansion, interventionist foreign policy, and culturally egalitarian state
projects undermine liberty and inherited social authority, while free markets
and local intermediary institutions can preserve order. It also states that
the historical paleoconservative-radical-libertarian coalition was brief and
internally divided over the necessity and role of state intervention.

This is a descriptive account of the movement’s own institutional expectations
and historical political strategy, not an endorsement of those claims. The
summary does not generalize the label to all libertarianism or treat the
coalition as a stable, internally uniform school.
