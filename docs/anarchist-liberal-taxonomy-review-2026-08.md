## Decision summary

This review updates the registry to `2026-08-taxonomy-v7`. It is a taxonomy and evidence pass, not a claim that any centroid or specialist result has been empirically validated.

- **Market Anarchism** is now an explicit provisional specialist in the `anarchist / market-anarchist` section. It is not a liberal primary. Mutualism, agorism, left-wing market anarchism, and anarcho-capitalism remain related but distinct variants.
- **Right-Libertarianism** remains the public name for the stable historical ID `market-right-libertarianism`; the awkward internal ID is retained only for research and link compatibility.
- **Mutualism** remains an independent `mutualist-anarchist` specialist with no Social / Communal Anarchism parent.
- **Anarcho-Primitivism** remains an anarchist specialist but is no longer represented as a child of Social / Communal Anarchism. Its anti-civilization focus is a separate organizational and technological axis.
- **Georgism** is no longer parented to Right-Libertarianism. Georgist libertarianism is a recognized left-libertarian form, while Georgism also includes land-rent and public-revenue proposals that do not require a right-libertarian state position.
- **Market Socialism** is no longer parented to Marxian Socialism. Its defining object is an economic-system design—social ownership with markets—which can be defended from Marxian, cooperative, democratic, or other traditions.
- **Black Nationalism** and **Pan-Africanism** are no longer children of Civic Nationalism. Their specialist profiles concern racialized/diasporic solidarity, anti-domination, self-determination, and transnational cooperation rather than civic membership in a state.
- **Ethnonationalism** leaves the primary pool and is now a modifier/identity-boundary result. The label can attach to different complete ideologies and does not determine one regime, economic system, or membership rule.
- **Civil-Libertarian Cosmopolitanism** is unretired as a modifier. It combines civil-libertarian and universalist commitments without pretending to be a complete primary ideology.

The review also adds four browseable research entries without promoting them into ordinary scoring: Market Anarchism, Third Way Social Democracy, Baʿthism / Arab Socialism, and Developmental Authoritarianism. Confucian Political Revival / Asian Values is context-only because the literature treats it as a contested discourse with both democratic and authoritarian interpretations.

## Evidence and interpretation

The Stanford Encyclopedia of Philosophy describes anarchism as internally diverse: individualist, libertarian, and anarcho-capitalist forms differ from socialist and communal forms over the source of authority, property, and social organization. That supports placing market-anarchist entries in the anarchist family while preserving the contested boundary around anarcho-capitalism. [SEP, “Anarchism”](https://plato.stanford.edu/archives/fall2024/entries/anarchism/)

The SEP account of libertarianism explicitly distinguishes right- and left-libertarianism by their treatment of natural resources and identifies Georgist libertarianism as a developed left-libertarian form. That is why Georgism cannot be treated as a required child of the Right-Libertarianism anchor. [SEP, “Libertarianism”](https://plato.stanford.edu/archives/sum2005/entries/libertarianism/)

Oxford’s liberalism overview distinguishes classical, libertarian, and high-liberal approaches by how they relate property and economic liberty to public goods, social minimums, and equal opportunity. The catalog therefore keeps Classical Liberalism, Social Liberalism, Right-Libertarianism, Ordoliberalism, and the narrower Market-Governance Liberalism use of `neoliberalism` separate rather than restoring a duplicate Market Liberal primary. [Oxford Research Encyclopedia, “Liberalism”](https://academic.oup.com/edited-volume/62239/chapter-abstract/550776269)

Cambridge scholarship describes market socialism as a family of institutional models combining social/public ownership with market coordination. Its economic-system focus does not require a Marxian parent, so the registry retains it as an independent provisional specialist. [Cambridge, “Investment planning in market socialism”](https://www.cambridge.org/core/books/abs/markets-and-democracy/investment-planning-in-market-socialism/0D417C0518C1C35C4AFC270084D5F05D)

Oxford research treats Black Nationalism as a heterogeneous field of racial consciousness, linked fate, solidarity, and collective action, and Pan-Africanism as a dynamic transnational solidarity and liberation tradition. These are not simply civic-national membership variants. [Oxford, “Black Nationalism”](https://academic.oup.com/edited-volume/61883/chapter-abstract/547804714), [Oxford, “Pan-Africanism”](https://academic.oup.com/edited-volume/61663/chapter-abstract/553395327)

The new Third Way entry is bounded by research on the transformation of social democracy around market principles, social investment, welfare reform, and responsibility. [Cambridge, “The Conversion of Social Democracy to the Third Way”](https://www.cambridge.org/core/books/global-neoliberal-capitalism-and-the-alternatives/conversion-of-social-democracy-to-the-third-way/3F798AE6232628B639BD2ED355D56464)

Baʿthism is added because Cambridge research distinguishes the founders’ Arab-nationalist and socialist constitutional claims from the later authoritarian practice of Syrian and Iraqi party-states. [Cambridge, “Free Elections versus Authoritarian Practices”](https://www.cambridge.org/core/books/arab-lefts/free-elections-versus-authoritarian-practices-what-baathists-fought-for/F3E94FDC2223EB7627E69010FFB17A50)

Developmental Authoritarianism is explicitly narrower than developmentalism in general. Cambridge’s developmental-state literature identifies state intervention, industrial policy, strong administrative capacity, and state–business coalitions, while also documenting variation in regime type and the limits of authoritarian developmental claims. [Cambridge, *Developmental States*](https://www.cambridge.org/core/books/developmental-states/1C537B3DBDDA2F1674C4C6F34E90B600)

Confucian Political Revival / Asian Values remains context-only because Cambridge research describes an active debate: regime-linked arguments have used Confucian values to challenge liberal democracy, while other scholarship argues that Confucian and regional traditions can support democratic interpretations. [Cambridge, “The Confucian Asian Values Thesis”](https://www.cambridge.org/core/books/confucianism-and-democratization-in-east-asia/confucian-asian-values-thesis/4CD5DC5B95018FA17774BADC4739B388)
