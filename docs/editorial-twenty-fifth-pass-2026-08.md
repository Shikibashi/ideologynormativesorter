# Descriptive construct-alignment pass — 2026-08

`2026-08-editorial-v25` corrects a small set of active descriptive items whose
empirical mechanism was being treated as evidence about a neighboring construct.
The pass preserves question IDs, layers, tiers, and source records. It changes
only the prompt or axis mapping where the correction is high-confidence.

## Corrections

- Coordination and property items (`q0012`, `q0027`, `q0029`, `q0030`, and
  `q0048`) now distinguish durable coordination, capture/entry barriers, and
  state-owned-enterprise accountability from general expertise claims.
- Housing, financial-entry, and standards items (`q0107`, `q0128`, `q0130`,
  and `q0147`) now use policy-targeting, entry, coordination, and market
  constructs that match their wording.
- Patent portfolios (`q0148`) now explicitly names cross-licensing, litigation
  leverage, entry, and competition rather than presenting a neutral list as a
  capture claim.
- Justice, religious-status, environmental-liability, and administrative
  complexity items (`q0188`, `q0190`, `q0191`, `q0248`, `q0269`, `q0307`, and
  `q0308`) now separate institutional incentives, legal enforcement, and
  distributional mechanisms.
- The Afghanistan reconstruction item (`q0328`) is limited to the documented
  local-adaptation/state-capacity finding. The democratic-backsliding item
  (`q0350`) and the GAO technology item (`q0368`) no longer treat institutional
  accountability findings as generic evidence about expert competence.

## Source boundaries

The NBER occupational-licensing study reports that, on the studied margin,
licensing raised wages and hours while reducing employment; it does not by
itself establish political capture. The OECD’s 2024 state-owned-enterprise
guidelines describe complex accountability chains and governance risks while
stating that ownership form alone does not determine performance. The FTC’s IP
marketplace report and WIPO materials describe licensing, cross-licensing,
litigation, and defensive portfolio uses without making them universal motives.

These sources support the item scopes and mechanisms, not the numeric centroids
or any respondent’s answer:

- [NBER occupational licensing](https://www.nber.org/papers/w26383)
- [OECD SOE governance guidelines](https://www.oecd.org/en/publications/oecd-guidelines-on-corporate-governance-of-state-owned-enterprises-2024_18a24f43-en/full-report/component-3.html)
- [FTC patent notice and remedies report](https://www.ftc.gov/reports/evolving-ip-marketplace-aligning-patent-notice-remedies-competition)
- [WIPO IP portfolio strategies](https://www.wipo.int/en/web/wipo-magazine/articles/ip-and-business-managing-ip-as-a-set-of-business-assets-36097)

The pass is a construct review, not human validation. Test–retest stability,
internal structure, criterion validity, subgroup fairness, and response-process
evidence remain required before interpreting any centroid as a validated
psychometric scale.
