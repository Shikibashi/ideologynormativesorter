# Specialist label source pass — 2026-08

Date: 2026-08-12

This pass reviewed the six highest-risk provisional specialist/context labels identified by the precision follow-up audit. It improves interpretive source coverage and wording boundaries; it does not promote any label into ordinary primary or modifier scoring and does not claim respondent validity.

## Remaining evidence gates

The catalog and source-contract tests can verify that the label records are present, scoped, and visible. They cannot establish that respondents distinguish the variants, that the centroids are valid, or that the modules are reliable. Those require cognitive interviews, pilot/retest data, construct coverage checks, subgroup invariance analysis, and qualified expert review of the experimental results.
