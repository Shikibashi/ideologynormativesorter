## Layer decision

Left-Wing Market Anarchism now has a descriptive summary for its characteristic account of the economy: legal privilege and state-backed corporate power distort markets and sustain domination, while a genuinely freed market could reduce exploitation through voluntary exchange and more egalitarian access. The summary keeps the current's internal disagreement over property, labor, and mutual provision.

This is presented as the current's theoretical expectation, not as an empirical finding that real-world markets will produce those results. The normative and prescriptive layers remain separate, and the label remains distinct from anarcho-capitalism.

## Source basis

- Gary Chartier and Charles W. Johnson, *Markets Not Capitalism*, describes left-wing market anarchism as pro-market and anti-capitalist, emphasizing the role of legal privilege and state-supported corporate power in shaping existing markets: https://theanarchistlibrary.org/library/gary-chartier-and-charles-w-johnson-markets-not-capitalism
- Gary Chartier, “Labor Exploitation: A Left-Libertarian Analysis,” in *Exploitation: Perspectives from Philosophy, Politics, and Economics*, argues that state interventions can enable private exploitation and that a freed market is expected to reduce labor exploitation: https://academic.oup.com/book/56140/chapter-abstract/442818231
