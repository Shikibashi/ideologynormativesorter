## Field and flag legend

Each row records the effective prompt, existing role, intended root and vNext facet, keyed semantic direction, discrimination target, nearest-root comparison set, risk flags, quiz depth, source/provenance quality, disposition, and coverage consequence. `AQ` means the universal agree-format acquiescence risk; `SD` is elevated social-desirability loading; `PA` is partisan or identity anchoring risk; `TA` is temporal or historical anchoring; `SK` is specialized-knowledge dependence; `DB` is double-barreling; `AMB` is unresolved wording ambiguity; `XL` is high cross-loading; `ASY` is exposure to an asymmetric root direction; `RED:*` is a redundancy or parallel-form cluster; `IPS` is ipsative option-level scoring; `MOD` is conditional Specialist-module evidence; `PROXY` means the current root mapping is being used for a named facet that remains directly unmeasured; `EV` means an evidence note is attached to a non-descriptive item and requires layer-boundary review.

`retain` means content-ready for the next empirical gate, not validated. `retain with minor edit` means the construct can survive a wording edit without changing the intended root. `rewrite` means the current item needs a new single-claim formulation. `replace` means the current item should not return under its present construct bundle; a replacement must be developed and reviewed before retirement. `empirical review required` means disposition depends on respondent data, especially dimensionality, item functioning, DIF/invariance, retest, or criterion evidence.
