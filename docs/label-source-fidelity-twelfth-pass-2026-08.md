# Label-source fidelity twelfth pass

Version: `2026-08-12`
Date: 2026-08-12

## Scope

This pass adds direct source records and boundary copy for five active labels
whose broad family sources were not enough to support their most specific public
interpretations: Christian Democrat, Social Democrat, Libertarian Socialism,
Fascist / Palingenetic Ultranationalist, and Civic Nationalist. It also corrects
the grammar of generated question-context notes. No centroid, axis weight,
question prompt, layer, or result eligibility changed.

## Source and copy decisions

- **Christian Democrat** now links Christian-democratic intellectual history
  and Cambridge research on subsidiarity. The copy distinguishes democratic
  Christian-democratic movements from theocracy and distributism.
- **Social Democrat** now links a direct Routledge term record and distinguishes
  contemporary reform of a predominantly capitalist mixed economy from
  democratic socialism and any single national model.
- **Libertarian Socialism** now receives a direct anarchism source and states
  that its anti-authoritarian use of “libertarian” is not the same as
  right-libertarian property politics.
- **Fascist / Palingenetic Ultranationalist** now links Roger Griffin’s
  Cambridge-published account of palingenetic ultranationalism and preserves
  the boundary against generic authoritarianism.
- **Civic Nationalist** now links Cambridge research on civic patriotism and
  states that civic criteria can remain contested, coercive, or exclusionary.

Sources explain the labels and their boundaries; they do not validate numeric
centroids, infer respondent identity, or establish psychometric performance.
