# Specialist question precision pass — August 2026

This pass narrows two respondent-facing specialist items that bundled related
institutional properties. The changes are measurement edits, not claims that
the affected traditions have one settled program. The experimental roster is
versioned as `2026-08-specialist-v7`; the question-context overlay is
`2026-08-question-context-v29`. Earlier administrations remain tied to their
previous versions.

## Changes

- `fm-fem-5` now asks specifically about reorganizing paid and unpaid labor,
  rather than asking one response to cover ownership, workplace power, and
  social reproduction. Its construct mapping is now only
  `class-social-reproduction`.
- `fm-mm-4` now asks specifically about confederal coordination among
  municipalities or regions, rather than requiring agreement with delegated,
  recallable, and non-sovereign institutional properties simultaneously.

These edits retain the existing prescriptive layers and axis compatibility.
They do not promote a specialist label, alter ordinary primary scoring, or
turn synthetic module fits into validation evidence.

## Research basis

- The [ILO summary of _Care work and care jobs for the future of decent work_](https://www.ilo.org/publications/care-work-and-care-jobs-future-decent-work-summary)
  treats paid and unpaid care work together and links their organization to
  gender inequalities in households and labor markets. That supports the
  narrower social-reproduction wording without treating ownership or one
  policy instrument as part of the same item.
- The [peer-reviewed discussion of democratic confederalism in _Problema_](https://revistas.juridicas.unam.mx/index.php/filosofia-derecho/article/view/18207)
  describes the project as a form of sociopolitical organization centered on
  self-organization and autonomous communities. That supports measuring the
  confederal coordination construct while leaving recall, delegation, and
  sovereignty as separate institutional details.

The cited literature supports construct framing and boundaries; it does not
validate the module’s candidate centroids or establish test-retest reliability.
