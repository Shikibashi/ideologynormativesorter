# Market anarchism taxonomy correction — 2026-08

This correction separates three things that the catalog had been presenting too closely:

- **Right-Libertarianism** is now the public name of the historical `market-right-libertarianism` primary ID. The old display name remains a searchable alias, so stored research IDs and historical links continue to resolve.
- **Market-anarchist specialist entries**—Anarcho-Capitalist, Agorist, and Left-Wing Market Anarchism—are grouped under the public `anarchist` family. This reflects that their defining claim is about stateless or anti-state institutional order, while their property and economic commitments differ. Anarcho-capitalism remains explicitly marked as contested within anarchist studies.
- **Mutualism** remains an anarchist specialist but is no longer assigned to the `social-anarchist` subfamily or parented to Social / Communal Anarchism. It is given its own `mutualist-anarchist` subfamily because its reciprocal exchange, mutual-credit, and property commitments bridge individualist, social, and market-anarchist currents without being a required subtype of any one of them.

The research basis is deliberately mixed rather than relying on a single ideological source. The Stanford Encyclopedia of Philosophy distinguishes right-libertarian anarcho-capitalism from left-market anarchism and separately describes socialist/communal anarchist concerns. A peer-reviewed study describes mutualism as a variety of anarchism organized around market exchange and mutual credit. Cambridge’s treatment of social anarchism also explicitly contrasts social anarchism with market-anarchist and anarcho-capitalist positions. These sources support the separation, while leaving the contested boundary around anarcho-capitalism visible rather than pretending the classification is universally settled.

- [Stanford Encyclopedia of Philosophy, “Anarchism”](https://plato.stanford.edu/entries/anarchism/)
