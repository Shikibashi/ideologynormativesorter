# Neoconservative layer guide

Date: 2026-08-11

## Scope

This focused editorial pass adds the missing descriptive layer summary for
`neoconservative`. Its direct term definition and normative or practical
summary already identified the modern U.S. current, but the descriptive layer
did not state its characteristic account of threats and international order.
No centroids, axis weights, question prompts, or scoring behavior changed.

## Layer decision

**Neoconservatism** now describes the expectation that authoritarian or
totalitarian threats and anti-communist adversaries endanger liberal-democratic
order, with national security, military power, and democracy promotion treated
as instruments of resistance. The wording preserves internal and historical
variation rather than presenting one fixed neoconservative foreign-policy
program or attributing the current to Leo Strauss by definition.

This is a descriptive account of a historical political current’s characteristic
argument, not an endorsement of military intervention or a claim that its
threat assessments have been empirically vindicated.
