# Confidence coverage pass — 2026-08

The Balanced profile now contains 206 items and the Full-depth profile contains 338. These coverage passes address the coverage bands shown in the public result, without presenting item-count coverage as validated psychometric reliability.

The previously sparse axes now meet or exceed the pragmatic moderate-coverage band used by the result screen: state-capacity confidence and expert confidence have eight direct Balanced items, cultural plasticity has nine, and the other audited axes have at least six. This is item-count coverage, not a claim that the axes are psychometrically validated.

Sixteen new single-axis questions (`q0427`–`q0442`) and two additional cultural-plasticity questions (`q0444`–`q0445`) were added in the first pass. The third coverage version (`2026-08-confidence-coverage-v3`) adds a construct-matched expert-confidence item (`q0473`) after unsupported cross-loadings were removed. The current fourth version adds paired single-axis items `q0474`–`q0479`: state-capacity confidence, expert confidence, and cultural plasticity each receive one positive and one negative observation. Existing direct items were promoted through `2026-08-editorial-v23`; quarantined “needs rewrite” items were not reactivated. Descriptive evidence for the new empirical items is versioned as `2026-08-descriptive-evidence-v4`, and the context/source overlay is `2026-08-question-context-v22`; v22 adds bespoke construct boundaries and source bundles for all six active statement-choice items without changing their prompts, options, or scoring fields. The later `2026-08-editorial-v24` pass separated civic membership from ancestry in `q0415` and religious tradition from inherited heritage in `q0405`; `2026-08-editorial-v25` removed unsupported cross-loadings from selected descriptive mechanisms and tightened four prompts; `2026-08-editorial-v26` corrected six residual source-to-construct mismatches, including the direction of the environmental-compliance item.

The source trail is claim-specific. Cambridge research informs the deliberative-knowledge item, state-capacity items, expertise, and accountability; the American Economic Association informs electoral information and accountability; OECD evidence-governance material informs expert transparency and contestability; Oxford research and Cambridge institutional research inform the norm-change items; and World Bank social-norm material informs the legal-change and norm-persistence items. The `2026-08-descriptive-evidence-v5` pass adds a second triangulating source to the remaining 40 single-source descriptive items without changing their operational evidence notes or weights. These sources support the scope notes and construct boundaries; they do not determine a respondent’s answer or establish scale reliability.

The result band remains a pragmatic answer-coverage heuristic. A future validation cohort still needs to assess internal consistency, test–retest stability, measurement error, differential item functioning, and whether the new items improve discrimination without creating systematic response burden.
