# Specialist source-fidelity pass — 2026-08

This pass tightens source scope for three narrow labels whose earlier records
relied too heavily on broad family baselines.

## Changes

- **Georgist Libertarian** now has the Oxford research record on George,
  land, wealth, community, and land-value taxation. The source supports the
  label’s land-rent mechanism and normative/prescriptive boundary; it does not
  define every Georgist or libertarian position.
- **Minarchism** now has Cambridge research on libertarianism and the state.
  The source places minimal-state arguments inside a dispute over rights,
  protection, redistribution, infrastructure, and the permissible scope of
  government. It does not imply that libertarianism has one settled state
  doctrine.
- **Anarcho-Communism** now has Cambridge historical research on Kropotkin and
  communist anarchism. The source supports the historical family boundary and
  descriptive lineage without treating one nineteenth-century current as a
  complete definition of contemporary anarcho-communism.
- **Dugin’s Fourth Political Theory** now separates a primary-text record of
  Dugin’s self-description from the Springer source used for scholarly
  criticism. The primary text supports what Dugin claims; it does not prove
  the project’s coherence or its claimed distance from fascism.

The source-scope map keeps family baselines from silently inheriting every
normative, descriptive, and prescriptive claim. These labels remain
provisional specialists and are not ordinary scored results.
