# v2 explainability

Explainability returns structured records for later view-model work. It does
not generate statements such as “You are...” or “Because you answered...”.

Contribution traces retain item and construct IDs, response state, normalized
value, mapping weight, salience factor, effective weight, signed contribution,
inclusion state, exclusion reason, and provenance references. Item wording and
citations remain resolvable from canonical content rather than being duplicated
inside every profile result.

Abstention is explicit. Missing, skipped, abstained, refused, salience-skipped,
inactive, and unavailable states are never described as disagreement. Gate
evaluations, evidence thresholds, and profile comparison records are exposed
as produced by the authoritative scorer.

Diagnostics are not reliability estimates, probabilities, identity claims,
psychological diagnoses, or statistical significance claims.
