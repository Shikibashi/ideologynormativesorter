# v2 Compatibility Content

Phase 13 does not include retired labels, compatibility labels, save/share compatibility records, participant IDs, or historical study labels in the active research corpus. Those remain outside the v2 research envelope and outside Worker acceptance.

Retired/compatibility records are migration-only or historical archive data. They may be read by a future explicit migration tool, but they cannot activate collection, alter scoring, or be treated as canonical research concepts.
