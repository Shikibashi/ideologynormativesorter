# Editorial eleventh pass: evidence-scope alignment

Version: `2026-08-editorial-v11`
Date: 2026-08-12

This pass narrows four active descriptive prompts so the respondent-facing
claim matches the scope of its evidence record. It does not change layer,
axis weights, response type, or specialist measurement status.

| Item    | Change                                                                                                                                                                                           | Research basis                                                                                                                                                                                                                                                                                    |
| ------- | ------------------------------------------------------------------------------------------------------------------------------------------------------------------------------------------------ | ------------------------------------------------------------------------------------------------------------------------------------------------------------------------------------------------------------------------------------------------------------------------------------------------- |
| `q0248` | Conditions the religion-and-law claim on jurisdictions where religious authorities have formal coercive legal power.                                                                             | [Pew Research Center: Many Countries Favor Specific Religions](https://www.pewresearch.org/religion/2017/10/03/many-countries-favor-specific-religions-officially-or-unofficially/) documents variation in official or favored religion and the legal privileges or restrictions that can follow. |
| `q0328` | Narrows the intervention/local-knowledge claim to the Afghanistan reconstruction.                                                                                                                | [SIGAR/USIP: Lessons from the Coalition](https://www.sigar.mil/Portals/147/Files/Reports/Lessons-Learned/SIGAR-16-59-LL.pdf) reports that success depended on local knowledge and buy-in and that donors struggled to tailor efforts to local conditions.                                         |
| `q0350` | Narrows the constitutional-erosion mechanism to documented democratic backsliding episodes and uses “sometimes.”                                                                                 | [International IDEA: Designing Resistance](https://www.idea.int/publications/catalogue/designing-resistance-democratic-institutions-and-threat-backsliding) provides the episode-level institutional scope used by the evidence record.                                                           |
| `q0368` | Replaces the unsupported general “repurposing” claim with the sourced observation that federal agencies use detection and monitoring tools across purposes and may lack complete privacy limits. | [GAO-25-107302](https://www.gao.gov/products/gao-25-107302) reports multi-purpose DHS use and gaps in policies covering collection and other privacy protections.                                                                                                                                 |

These edits are editorial scope corrections, not findings that establish causal
effects or validate the quiz’s psychometric performance. The question-bank
fingerprint is therefore bumped, and historical responses retain their prior
bank metadata.
