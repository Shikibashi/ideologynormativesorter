# Editorial ninth pass — August 2026

## Scope

This pass covers the active core bank and the two respondent-facing specialist modules after the all-item context overlay.

It makes four compound prompts single-construct policy preferences while preserving their question IDs and existing axis weights:

- `q0075`: supply-side barriers before new tax-funded benefits;
- `q0215`: lawful immigration pathways during housing and labour-market adjustment;
- `q0274`: adult exit from legal or economic dependency;
- `q0314`: legal barriers to low-carbon deployment.

The pass also revises the ipsative wording of `sq02` and `sq13` so their alternatives represent clearer primary principles rather than near-paraphrased distributive positions. Statement-choice options remain outside common-scale reliability estimates.

## Specialist descriptive evidence

The three active descriptive specialist items now have operational scopes and public sources:

- `fm-fem-2` uses the World Bank Women, Business and the Law 2024 legal-framework versus implementation comparison and ILO care-work material. The evidence distinguishes formal sex-neutral law from implementation and lived institutional outcomes; it does not claim one global measure of patriarchy.
- `fm-fem-4` uses ILO care-work and time-use research to separate unpaid care hours, household labour, labour-force participation, earnings, and progression rather than treating them as one causal coefficient.
- `fm-id-13` uses UN Indigenous Peoples materials and ILO Convention 169 guidance to compare formal citizenship with collective land rights, participation, consultation, treaty implementation, and documented dispossession or relocation.

These sources provide claim context, not answer keys or psychometric validation.

## Review provenance

Every active core and respondent-facing specialist item now has explicit `approved` review metadata. This pass adds `2026-08-editorial-v9` metadata to previously unversioned active items, while items with an earlier review record retain that provenance unless this pass changes them. Legacy data-only module questions remain outside this sign-off because no respondent-facing registry consumes them.
