# Question-context twenty-second pass

This pass uses `2026-08-question-context-v27` and adds bespoke context/source
records for the final 41 generic active core questions. The scope covers state
legitimacy, markets and planning, intellectual property, crime and justice,
race/ethnicity and multiculturalism, productive ownership, and land-value
taxation. It does not change prompts, response scales, axis weights, or label
assignments.

## Scope

- State legitimacy: `q0015`, `q0017`–`q0019`.
- Markets and planning: `q0053`–`q0055`, `q0058`, and `q0059`.
- Intellectual property and information: `q0141`, `q0144`–`q0146`, `q0153`, and `q0155`–`q0157`, `q0159`.
- Crime, policing, and justice: `q0181`–`q0186`, `q0195`, `q0197`–`q0199`.
- Race, ethnicity, and multiculturalism: `q0281`–`q0285`, `q0293`–`q0296`, and `q0299`.
- Ownership and land value: `q0407`, `q0408`, and `q0424`.

The framing keeps normative claims about rights, standing, and legitimacy
separate from prescriptive preferences about institutions and descriptive
claims about mechanisms. In particular, decentralized authority is not
assumed to be well-coordinated, open copying is not assumed to eliminate all
creator interests, punishment is not treated as the only form of justice,
group-conscious remedies are not equated with collective guilt, and a land
value tax is not treated as a complete theory of public finance.

## Source basis

The records use the existing source catalog, including the [SEP entry on
Federalism](https://plato.stanford.edu/entries/federalism/), [Democracy](https://plato.stanford.edu/entries/democracy/index.html),
[Markets](https://plato.stanford.edu/entries/markets/), [Property](https://plato.stanford.edu/entries/property/index.html),
[Intellectual Property](https://www.wipo.int/about-ip/en/), [WIPO limitations and
exceptions](https://www.wipo.int/en/web/copyright/limitations/index), the [SEP
entry on Multiculturalism](https://plato.stanford.edu/entries/multiculturalism/),
the [International Covenant on Civil and Political
Rights](https://2covenants.ohchr.org/About-ICCPR.html), the [SEP entry on
Legal Punishment](https://plato.stanford.edu/entries/legal-punishment/), the [World Bank
land-policy research](https://www.worldbank.org/en/publication/land-policies-africa),
and the [SEP entry on Distributive
Justice](https://plato.stanford.edu/entries/justice-distributive/index.html).

These sources support definitions, rights boundaries, and cautious framing;
they do not establish psychometric validity or determine a respondent’s
answer.
