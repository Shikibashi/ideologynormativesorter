# Label-source fidelity: fifteenth pass (2026-08)

This pass extends bespoke catalog sourcing for provisional specialist traditions whose names are easily mistaken for broad parent labels or neighboring schools. The sources support interpretive definitions and boundaries; they do not validate the app’s centroids, respondent assignments, or claims about real-world efficacy.

## Added source coverage

- **Council communism** — Cambridge research on workers’ control and council democracy. This keeps councilism distinct from generic Marxism–Leninism and from every workplace-democracy proposal.
- **Degrowth Green** — Oxford scholarship on planned reductions in energy and resource use in high-consuming economies. The note distinguishes degrowth from recession, austerity, and generic environmentalism.
- **Deep Ecology** — Oxford environmental-political theory on nonhuman value and the critique of anthropocentrism, plus a handbook treatment distinguishing deep ecology from eco-Marxist/ecosocialist and primitivist strands.
- **Ecosocialism** — the Oxford handbook’s eco-Marxist strand of radical environmentalism. This is a family boundary source, not a claim that all ecosocialists share one policy program.
- **Absolute Monarchism** — Cambridge historical research on absolutism as a contested theory of concentrated sovereignty, distinct from constitutional monarchy and generic executive strength.
- **Maoism** — Cambridge intellectual history distinguishing Maoism as a historically specific development of Mao’s later thought from the whole Chinese revolution or Marxism–Leninism in general.
- **Trotskyism** — Cambridge historiography documenting a divided movement whose programmatic debates and relation to Stalinism changed across time.
- **Participism** — a peer-reviewed critique of Albert and Hahnel’s participatory-economics model. It is used for model definition and boundary context, not as evidence that the model is feasible or effective.
- **Individualist Anarchism** — Wiley reference scholarship on the historical family’s focus on individual autonomy and anti-authoritarianism, without equating it with anarcho-capitalism or social anarchism.

## Scope discipline

Historical and critical sources are intentionally limited to `definition`, `descriptive`, and `boundary` where they do not directly establish a label’s normative or prescriptive claims. The label remains provisional and is not promoted into ordinary primary or modifier scoring by this source addition.
