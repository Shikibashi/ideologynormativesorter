# Ideology and helper tenth pass

Date: 2026-08-11

## Scope

This post-release pass reviewed unfamiliar result names that still had no term guide, layer explainers that falsely implied no curated practical doctrine was available, and two high-use questionnaire helpers. It changes explanatory copy only. No centroid, axis weight, question prompt, question selection, or score changes.

## Term-guide corrections

- Added direct guides for Mutualism, Agorism, Welfare Chauvinism, Participism, Panarchism, Liquid Democracy, Ecomodernism, and Ecosocialism.
- Configured each specific guide to appear alone instead of being crowded by a generic parent-family definition.
- Preserved scope cautions: Panarchism is identified as niche, Participism refers specifically to participatory economics, and Liquid Democracy is defined by delegable proxy voting rather than by a general preference for participation.

## Layer corrections

- Mutualism now has a practical summary covering mutual credit, cooperative exchange, possession or use-based claims, and federated voluntary institutions.
- Ecomodernism now has a practical summary covering technological innovation, resource-efficient infrastructure, conservation, and active public institutions.
- Ecosocialism now has a practical summary covering social ownership and democratic planning around need, equality, and ecological limits.

These are documentary summaries of the catalog labels. Empirical-belief layers remain explicitly uncurated where the label does not establish one common testable belief.

## Questionnaire-helper corrections

- **Exit** no longer assumes every exit is a switch between commercial providers; it now covers relationships, organizations, jurisdictions, and providers and distinguishes leaving from trying to change an institution from within.
- **Direct action** now gives recognizable examples and states that the concept is not necessarily violent.
