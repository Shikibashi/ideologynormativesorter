# Label source-fidelity pass — 2026-08

This pass adds claim-matched background records for eleven provisional or
narrow catalog entries. They remain browsable specialists or context entries;
the new sources do not promote them into ordinary scoring or validate their
centroids.

## Added records

- **Anarcho-Capitalism:** a Cambridge political-science treatment of
  Rothbardian state theory, with its critique kept separate from the movement's
  self-description.
- **Mutualism:** Cambridge intellectual-history context for Proudhonian
  mutualism and its position between socialism and anarchism.
- **Anarcho-Primitivism:** a SAGE political-philosophy treatment of primitivism
  as a critique of progress, modernity, and technological culture.
- **Syndicalism and Anarcho-Syndicalism:** comparative Cambridge labor history
  and a Taylor & Francis historical study of changing anarcho-syndicalist
  relations with Bolshevism.
- **Agorism:** Konkin's primary text, marked as a movement source rather than
  scholarship; it documents self-description but cannot establish efficacy.
- **Political Islam:** Oxford Bibliographies distinguishes modern political
  mobilization in the name of Islam from Islam, revival, and Muslim political
  activity generally.
- **Integralism:** Cambridge research distinguishes Catholic integralism from
  Christian nationalism generally and from fascism.
- **Juche:** Oxford scholarship distinguishes North Korean leader-centered
  ideology from generic self-reliance or generic socialism.
- **National Socialism:** the United States Holocaust Memorial Museum provides
  historical context for Nazi racial ideology, antisemitism, expansion, and
  genocide.
- **Neoreaction:** SAGE situates the niche current in right-accelerationist and
  post-libertarian critiques of state-centered liberal governance.

Each record carries only the claim scopes supported by that source. These
entries continue to display their provisional or context-only status, and the
remaining requirement for specialist promotion is respondent evidence:
construct coverage, reliability, criterion interpretation, retest behavior,
and fairness review.
