## Layer decision

National Socialism now has a descriptive summary that reports recurring Nazi ideological claims: racial hierarchy, leader-centered state power, and territorial expansion were presented as means of securing and regenerating a racially defined German national community. The summary explicitly marks those claims as ideological commitments rather than valid findings about human societies.

The descriptive layer therefore does not normalize, endorse, or empirically validate Nazi racial doctrine, dictatorship, antisemitism, or expansionism. The existing direct definition and normative layer remain separate: the direct definition identifies the historical ideology, while the normative layer states its commitments.
