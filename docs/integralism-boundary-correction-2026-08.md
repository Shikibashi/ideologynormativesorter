# Integralism boundary correction — 2026-08

This pass corrects a catalog relationship error without changing the
`integralism` centroid or any question mapping.

## Correction

- `Integralism` remains the stable public ID and name for compatibility.
- `Catholic Integralism` is now a clarifying alias because the label’s
  definition is specifically about the Catholic integralist tradition.
- `Clerical Fascism` is no longer listed as an Integralism subtype. The
  catalog’s existing caution and explainer correctly treat clerical fascism as
  a historically specific alliance or fusion involving some integralist
  actors, not as a synonym or required branch of integralism.

## Evidence boundary

- The Oxford Journal of Law and Religion introduction identifies the new
  Catholic integralism as a religious anti-liberal tradition whose ideal
  political order subordinates the state to the church. It supports the
  definition and normative boundary, not a claim about every historical
  movement using the word *integralism*:
  [Introduction to OJLR Special Issue: The New Catholic Integralism and
  Religious Anti-Liberalism](https://academic.oup.com/ojlr/advance-article-abstract/doi/10.1093/ojlr/rwag013/8735948)
- Cambridge research treats Catholic integralism as one distinct form within
  the Christian-nationalism field and does not make it identical to all
  Christian nationalism or fascism:
  [The varieties of American Christian nationalism](https://www.cambridge.org/core/journals/politics-and-religion/article/varieties-of-american-christian-nationalism/B78994104CA1CCBBAC34A20E3AA2A980)
