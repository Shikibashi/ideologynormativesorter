# Editorial seventeenth pass — 2026-08

This pass narrows twelve active descriptive prompts after checking whether the
published source supported the original wording or only a more specific
mechanism. It changes prompt wording, evidence notes, and source/context
records; it does not change the layer, response type, or axis weights.

## Changes

- `q0012`: scoped polycentric cooperation to documented common-pool settings
  and named the mechanisms in the Ostrom evidence.
- `q0027`: removed the unsupported “artificial scarcity” qualifier and
  separated conflict reduction from the distribution of access and power.
- `q0030`: separated state ownership from managerial hierarchy and
  accountability rather than asserting a common transfer of control.
- `q0047` and `q0048`: replaced rhetorical market/planning contrasts with the
  narrower information-transmission and information-access mechanisms.
- `q0148`: separated patent disclosure from portfolio uses such as licensing,
  cross-licensing, litigation strategy, and defense.
- `q0171`: replaced a broad panic-and-persistence story with the documented
  institutional reason for sunset clauses and periodic review.
- `q0188`: separated law-enforcement activity measures from safety, trust, and
  legitimacy outcomes.
- `q0190`: made the forfeiture incentive conditional on access to proceeds or
  equitable-sharing payments.
- `q0269`: replaced “administratively invisible” with measurable household
  classification, eligibility, and net-transfer differences.
- `q0308`: replaced the directional incumbent claim with a measurable
  compliance-cost incidence question.
- `q0348`: scoped information-acquisition incentives to low-salience settings.
