# Ideology label editorial review — 2026-08

This is the full editorial disposition for the 118-label catalog in src/data/labels.ts. It reviews user-facing name, description, family/subfamily scope, usage/caution context, and fit with the three-layer instrument. It does not validate numeric centroids against respondents and does not authorize automatic label promotion from self-reported ideology names.

## Rubric

Each label was checked for:

- canonical scope: ideology, school, movement, modifier, policy proposal, or governance concept;
- neutral wording that describes commitments without treating the label as praise or condemnation;
- separation from neighboring traditions and from religion, party identity, policy, or tactic;
- fit with normative, descriptive, and prescriptive interpretation;
- a visible caveat when the term is contested, niche, historically specific, internally diverse, or easy to conflate.

The review preserves existing label IDs and centroids. This continuation adds Socialist / Marxist Feminism as a specialist catalog label tied to the existing construct-matched feminist follow-up rather than to the primary scoring pool.

## Three-layer editorial rule

A label’s summary is read separately in each layer:

- **Normative:** what the tradition treats as legitimate, just, valuable, or permissible.
- **Descriptive:** what it expects about institutions, incentives, culture, and consequences.
- **Prescriptive:** what it recommends doing under current constraints, including reform, strategy, and institutional design.

A label can be coherent in one layer and diverge in another. The result UI therefore keeps the label description secondary to the three profiles and shows the layer-specific interpretation separately.

## Deferred precision work

These are not copy defects that should be hidden by rewriting. They require new measurement content, variants, or respondent evidence before centroid changes:

- Accelerationism: left, right/unconditional, and technology-centered variants.
- Political Zionism: liberal, labor/socialist, religious, revisionist, and other variants.
- Hindutva: civilizational, religious-national, and party-political variants.
- Islamic Democratic Constitutionalism: constitutional, party-political, and majoritarian variants.
- Cyberocratic Governance: algorithmic administration, cybernetic planning, and technocratic information governance.
- Techno-Anarchist / Crypto-Anarchist: crypto-anarchist, peer-to-peer commons, and broader techno-libertarian variants.
- Ecomodernism: technology-enabled ecological decoupling versus generic technocracy or market environmentalism.
- Revolutionary State Socialist: separation from Marxist-Leninist, Maoist, council-communist, and other revolutionary-socialist profiles.
- Republicanism: continued UI clarification that this means civic republicanism and non-domination, not a contemporary party label.
- Right- and left-wing populism: retain thin-ideology language and avoid treating either as a complete doctrine.
- Multiculturalism and Indigenism: dedicated construct coverage before promotion or subtype multiplication.

This ledger should be updated when a label is renamed, split, retired, or re-mapped; it should not be treated as a substitute for scholarly review of future additions.
