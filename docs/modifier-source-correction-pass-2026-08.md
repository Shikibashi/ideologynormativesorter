# Modifier source-correction pass — August 2026

This pass corrects three modifier entries whose only inherited source was too indirect for their public definitions.

- **Theocratic Politics** now cites Oxford’s [*Secularism in Political Philosophy*](https://academic.oup.com/edited-volume/62239/chapter-abstract/550724223), which distinguishes theocracy from secularism through state ends, institutions, law, legitimacy, and jurisdiction. Religious belief or a religious party is not treated as sufficient by itself.
- **Eco-Authoritarianism** now cites the Cambridge/British Journal of Political Science article [*Democracy, Autocracy, and Everything in Between*](https://www.cambridge.org/core/journals/british-journal-of-political-science/article/abs/democracy-autocracy-and-everything-in-between-how-domestic-institutions-affect-environmental-protection/919F89325DCF95477795D0539F89C26C), which treats eco-authoritarianism as a contested institutional argument and does not establish authoritarian environmental superiority.
- **Internationalism** now cites [*Philosophy and Internationalist Ethics*](https://academic.oup.com/manchester-scholarship-online/book/19999/chapter-abstract/178948448), which supports its treatment as a diverse cross-border political ideology rather than a synonym for liberalism, cosmopolitanism, or world government.

The records are scoped to definition, descriptive interpretation, and boundary. The existing family baselines still supply broader normative or prescriptive context where the catalog displays it; these sources do not validate modifier centroids or respondent measurement.
